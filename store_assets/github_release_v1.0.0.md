# MotoCents v1.0.0 🏍️💰

Welcome to the initial release of **MotoCents** — a production-grade, offline-first app designed for tracking vehicle fuel economy, maintenance services, daily running costs, and sensitive documents with authenticated encryption.

---

### 🍏 For iPhone & iPad Users (Instant Install — No Computer or Sideloading Required!)

iPhone users can run MotoCents directly on their device without needing a Mac, TestFlight, or sideloading tools:

🌐 **Open the Web App**: **[https://an-nihal.github.io/MotoCents](https://an-nihal.github.io/MotoCents)**

#### 📲 How to Install on iPhone / iPad:
1. Open **[https://an-nihal.github.io/MotoCents](https://an-nihal.github.io/MotoCents)** in **Safari** on your iPhone.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with the upward arrow at the bottom of the screen).
3. Scroll down the menu and tap **Add to Home Screen** (হোম স্ক্রিনে যোগ করুন).
4. Tap **Add** in the top-right corner.
5. **MotoCents** will now appear on your iPhone home screen with the official app icon and launch in full-screen standalone mode (no Safari address bar) with offline support!

*(Advanced iOS users who prefer to sideload via AltStore, Sideloadly, or a personal developer certificate can also download **`MotoCents.ipa`** from the Assets list below).*

### ✨ Highlights & Features

* ⛽ **Smart Fuel & Mileage Tracking**: Accurate Full-to-Full tank mileage calculation (`km/L`, `L/100km`, `MPG`) with live 2-of-3 auto-reconciliation (Quantity, Price per unit, Total Cost).
* 🔧 **Maintenance & Service Logs**: Track routine servicing, oil changes, and parts replacement with dual distance (km) and elapsed-days reminders.
* 🔒 **Encrypted Document Vault**: Store driving licenses, registration cards, insurance, and receipts protected by **AES-256-GCM** hardware-backed encryption and biometric unlock (Fingerprint / Face ID).
* 📊 **Cost Analytics & Station Insights**: Monthly spend breakdowns, fuel price trends, and station comparison to discover where you get the best mileage.
* 🛣️ **Trip Cost Calculator**: Estimate one-way or round-trip fuel costs and split highway tolls easily across passengers.
* 📄 **Buyer-Ready Reports**: Export comprehensive PDF Vehicle History reports, multi-sheet Excel (`.xlsx`) workbooks, and CSV logs.
* 🌐 **Bilingual (English & বাংলা)**: Full support for English and Bengali with native numeral formatting (`০, ১, ২...`) and multi-currency support (`BDT ৳`, `USD $`, `EUR €`, `INR ₹`, etc.).
* 🛡️ **100% Offline & Private**: Zero cloud telemetry, no sign-in required, and no third-party tracking. All data remains exclusively on your device.
