Privacy Policy for MotoCents

Effective Date: November 2026

MotoCents ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data.

1. Zero Data Collection & Offline Storage

MotoCents operates as an offline-first, local-only application. We do not operate remote servers, user tracking databases, or analytics services.

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register an account, log in, or provide an email address to use MotoCents.
  • Local Storage: All vehicle records, fuel entries, maintenance logs, and expense data are stored directly on your physical device using SQLite.
  • Zero Cloud Telemetry: We do not track, collect, or transmit your logs, mileage, financial costs, or location to any external servers.

2. Encrypted Document Vault & Biometrics

The app allows you to store sensitive vehicle documents (e.g., driver's license, registration, insurance, and receipts). These documents are encrypted directly on your device using AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption. Decryption keys are stored strictly in your device's secure hardware keystore. Biometric verification (fingerprint or face unlock) is handled directly by your operating system; MotoCents never accesses or stores your biometric data.

3. Device Permissions

MotoCents may request the following device permissions solely to provide on-device features:

  • Camera: Used exclusively to photograph physical receipts or vehicle documents to attach to your records.
  • Photo Library / Storage: Used exclusively to allow you to import receipt images or save exported PDF/Excel reports to your device.
  • Notifications: Used solely to schedule local on-device service reminders (e.g., upcoming oil changes).

4. Third-Party Services

The app does not incorporate third-party advertising SDKs, tracking frameworks, or cloud analytics tools.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via your official support channel or GitHub repository.
