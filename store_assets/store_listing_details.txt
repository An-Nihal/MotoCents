[DETAILED DESCRIPTION]
MotoCents is your precision vehicle companion designed to track fuel efficiency, maintenance history, daily expenses, and sensitive vehicle documents with 100% offline privacy and bank-grade encryption.

Whether you drive a motorcycle, car, microbus, or truck, MotoCents gives you complete control over your vehicle's running costs and total cost of ownership (TCO).

🌟 KEY FEATURES:

⛽ SMART FUEL LOGGING & REAL-TIME MILEAGE
• Full-to-Full fuel tank calculation for accurate fuel efficiency (km/L, L/100km, MPG).
• Live 2-of-3 Auto-Calculation: Enter any two of Quantity, Price, or Total Cost, and the third is calculated instantly.
• Trip meter or total odometer tracking with odometer consistency checks.
• Fuel efficiency drop alerts and historical fuel price trend analysis.

🔧 SERVICE & MAINTENANCE TRACKER
• Log oil changes, brake pads, chain adjustments, tyre rotations, and general repairs.
• Multi-item service invoices with part costs and workshop labor separation.
• Dual service reminders based on both distance (e.g. every 3,000 km) and elapsed days (e.g. every 90 days).

🔒 ENCRYPTED DOCUMENT & RECEIPT VAULT
• Protect your driving license, vehicle registration smart card, tax token, insurance, and receipts.
• Authenticated AES-256-GCM encryption with device-hardware key storage.
• Protected by device biometric authentication (Fingerprint / Face Unlock / PIN).
• "Quick Show" mode for fast roadside police checks without exposing the rest of your app.

📊 COMPREHENSIVE COST & MILEAGE ANALYTICS
• Detailed interactive monthly spend graphs, category breakdowns, and fuel cost per kilometer.
• Station insights: Compare fuel stations to see where you get the best mileage and lowest rates.
• Built-in Trip Cost Calculator: Split trip fuel and highway tolls across passengers.

📄 BUYER-READY REPORTS & EXPORT
• Generate buyer-ready, professional PDF Vehicle History Reports with complete maintenance history.
• Export raw data to Excel (.xlsx) and CSV sheets.
• Encrypted single-file backups (.fslbackup) to move your data between devices securely.

🌐 BILINGUAL SUPPORT (ENGLISH & বাংলা)
• Seamless switching between English and Bengali (বাংলা).
• Supports local currencies (BDT ৳, USD $, EUR €, INR ₹, GBP £, and more).
• Locale-aware Bengali numerals (০, ১, ২...).

🛡️ 100% OFFLINE & PRIVATE
• No sign-up or login required.
• Zero telemetry, zero third-party cloud tracking.
• All database records and encrypted vault files reside exclusively on your physical device.
