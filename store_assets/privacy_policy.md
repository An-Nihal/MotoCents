# Privacy Policy for MotoCents

**Effective Date:** November 2026  
**Status:** 100% Offline & Private Application

MotoCents ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data and on-device storage.

---

## 1. Zero Data Collection & Offline Storage

MotoCents operates as an **offline-first, local-only** application. We do not operate remote servers, user tracking databases, or analytics services.

* **No Account Required:** You do not need to register an account, log in, or provide an email address, phone number, or name to use MotoCents.
* **Local Storage:** All vehicle records, fuel entries, maintenance logs, and expense data are stored directly on your physical device using SQLite.
* **Zero Cloud Telemetry:** We do not track, collect, or transmit your logs, mileage, financial costs, or location to any external servers or third parties.

---

## 2. Encrypted Document Vault & Biometrics

The app allows you to store sensitive vehicle documents (such as your driver's license, vehicle registration smart card, tax token, insurance, and receipts). 

* **Hardware-Backed Encryption:** These documents are encrypted directly on your device using **AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption**.
* **Key Protection:** Decryption keys are stored strictly in your device's secure hardware keystore / keychain via Android Keystore.
* **Biometric Authentication:** Biometric verification (Fingerprint or Face Unlock) is handled directly by your device operating system via standard OS security prompts. MotoCents never accesses, collects, or stores your biometric data.

---

## 3. Device Permissions

MotoCents requests only the minimum permissions necessary to function on your device:

* **Camera:** Used solely when you choose to take a photo of a physical fuel receipt or vehicle document to attach to a log.
* **Photo Library / Storage:** Used solely to let you pick an existing image or document from your device, or to save exported PDF and Excel reports to your Downloads folder.
* **Notifications:** Used solely to schedule local, on-device maintenance reminders (e.g., upcoming oil changes based on elapsed time). No remote push notifications are used.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

The app does **not** include:
* Advertising SDKs or ad networks.
* Third-party tracking or behavioral analytics tools.
* External data brokers or cloud sync services.

---

## 5. Contact & Support

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy or MotoCents, please reach out via our GitHub repository or support channels.
